Privacy Policy for Welding Memo

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Welding Memo is a private application operated by IVA Dev for authorized users known to the operator.

Google user data accessed

The application may access Google Drive files and folders, Google Sheets spreadsheets and their contents, and Google Apps Script projects and deployments that the authorized user permits it to access.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide Welding Memo functionality, including reading and updating memo records, working with related drawings and files, making backups, performing migrations, and maintaining Apps Script code and deployments.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored on the authorized local workstation. Welding Memo does not sell Google user data. Google user data is not shared with third parties except where the user explicitly directs an operation or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Local credentials and local data copies are retained only as needed for the owner's automation and development workflow. The authorized owner can revoke Google access from their Google Account permissions and can delete local credentials at any time.

Security

Credentials are excluded from source control and are intended to remain on the authorized workstation. Access should be granted only by the Google account owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to ivadev.info@gmail.com.
