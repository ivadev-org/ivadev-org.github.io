Welding Memo

Welding Memo is a private productivity application maintained by IVA Dev. It helps its authorized owner manage welding memo data, reference drawings, and related automation backed by Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Google Apps Script.

Google data access

The local automation may request access to Google Drive, Google Sheets, and Google Apps Script so it can read, update, back up, migrate, and maintain the owner's Welding Memo resources. Access is used only for functionality explicitly initiated or configured by the authorized owner.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the authorized local workstation and are not published on this website or committed to public source repositories.
